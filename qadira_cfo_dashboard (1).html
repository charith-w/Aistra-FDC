How lift is measured
Each broker-sourced buyer's pre-contact activity trail — did they visit a sales centre, view a listing directly, or engage a Qadira channel before the broker's first logged touch — is compared against the equivalent profile of direct-channel buyers in the same period. A broker whose buyers show no prior Qadira contact scores high lift; one whose buyers repeatedly match people already in the pipeline scores low. This is a comparison against a stated baseline, not a black-box score.

Pricing Methodology

Why active projects, not Salesforce seats
The basis, and why
Priced on the active project — not the seat, not the user. Qadira already owns 22 unused Salesforce seats and a dormant AI add-on; pricing on seats would repeat the exact mistake that's souring the CFO on this vendor relationship already. Each project is its own pipeline and inventory pool with its own calibration — unit mix, price band and broker footprint differ project to project — so cost-to-serve scales with project count, which is also the variable the "four projects going to eight" question is actually asking about.
IncludedNot Included
  • All four modules, per active project
  • Weekly digest + exception alerts
  • Monthly 30-minute review call
  • Recalibration as data accumulates
  • Compatibility check after any flagged Northbay release, at no extra charge
  • Any change to Salesforce configuration (Northbay's rate card)
  • Reading the internal operations WhatsApp group
  • Training beyond the one included session
  • A different asset class, e.g. villas — priced as a small calibration add-on

The Business Case, Restated

Estimated on Qadira's numbers, not ours — arithmetic in the pack
The three problems Layla named cost an estimated AED 4.5M–5.5M a year [EST] — a forecasting miss's financing cost, unpriced broker commission, and stuck-inventory carrying cost. Against a year-one Groundtruth cost of ≈AED 397,000, that's an unadjusted return of roughly 11–14×. Because cost-avoidance claims are the easiest kind of ROI to oversell — and this CFO already regrets one vendor purchase — the number to lead with is the risk-adjusted one: ~530% year-one ROI even if the estimates are only half right, with payback inside the first two quarters of full deployment.
