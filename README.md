# Groundtruth

## Operations Intelligence for Qadira Developments

Groundtruth is an operations intelligence layer designed to turn existing Salesforce data into clear, actionable management signals.

Instead of requiring management to search through dashboards and reports, Groundtruth identifies the most important changes in the business and delivers them through a simple weekly intelligence digest and exception alerts.

The solution is designed around one principle:

> **Tell management what matters — without asking the sales team to enter more data.**

# The Business Problem

Qadira already has Salesforce and significant operational data.

The challenge is not the absence of information. The challenge is turning that information into timely management decisions.

Groundtruth addresses four key questions:

1. **What is the pipeline actually likely to close?**
2. **Which deals are losing momentum right now?**
3. **Which brokers are creating incremental demand?**
4. **Where is inventory moving too slowly?**

The solution converts existing Salesforce activity into a small number of explainable signals that management can act on.

---

# The Groundtruth Solution

Groundtruth operates outside the Salesforce user interface and reads data through the standard Salesforce API.

It is designed to require:

* No new Salesforce fields
* No additional sales-team data entry
* No new daily workflow for the sales team
* No changes to Northbay's Salesforce configuration
* No replacement of Salesforce
* No replacement of Northbay

The operating model is:

# Core Intelligence Modules

## 1. Pipeline Reality Score

The Pipeline Reality Score provides a behavioural re-forecast of what is actually likely to close.

It uses signals already captured by Salesforce, including:

* Deal velocity
* Activity recency
* Stage-change timestamps
* Historical stage-to-close conversion
* Email and call activity metadata

The prototype compares the Salesforce / CRM forecast with the Groundtruth reality score.

### Prototype example

| Metric                    |     Value |
| ------------------------- | --------: |
| CRM Forecast              | AED 61.4M |
| Groundtruth Reality Score | AED 43.9M |

The objective is not simply to produce another forecast.

It is to make the difference between the CRM forecast and behavioural reality visible and explainable.

---

## 2. Deal Risk Radar

Deal Risk Radar identifies opportunities that are losing momentum.

Rather than presenting a generic AI risk score, each deal is accompanied by the specific signal that triggered the alert.

Potential signals include:

* Extended inactivity
* Stalled stage progression
* Declining activity
* Missed milestones
* Delayed follow-up
* Changes in engagement patterns

The result is a prioritised list of deals that require management or sales-team attention.

---

## 3. Broker Net-Lift Ranking

Qadira works with approximately 90 agencies.

Raw broker sales volume does not necessarily indicate incremental value.

Groundtruth therefore evaluates brokers against the direct-channel baseline.

The analysis considers:

* Previous buyer interaction with Qadira
* Sales-centre activity
* Direct listing engagement
* Existing pipeline activity
* Buyer profile
* Unit type
* Price band
* Project
* Broker's first recorded interaction

The objective is to distinguish between:

**Demand generated by the broker**

and

**Demand that may already have existed without the broker.**

This allows Qadira to evaluate broker performance based on incremental lift rather than volume alone.

---

## 4. Unit Velocity Map

The Unit Velocity Map identifies areas of slow-moving inventory.

The analysis can be viewed across:

* Project
* Tower / Block
* Floor
* Unit type
* Days on market

The prototype focuses on the Palm Vista Towers, Tower B floors 7–11 example.

This allows management to identify concentrated inventory problems and investigate potential causes.

# The Executive Intelligence Experience

Groundtruth is designed as a **push-based intelligence product**, rather than another dashboard that management needs to remember to open.

The intended weekly experience is:

```text
Monday Morning

      ↓

Pipeline Reality
What is actually likely to close?

      ↓

Deal Risk
Which opportunities are losing momentum?

      ↓

Broker Net-Lift
Which brokers are generating incremental demand?

      ↓

Unit Velocity
Where is inventory getting stuck?

      ↓

Management Review
What requires action this week?
```

The primary committed delivery channel is a one-page email/PDF digest.

WhatsApp Business can operate as an additional delivery channel, subject to template approval and associated messaging requirements.

The Groundtruth WhatsApp experience is intended as a one-way executive delivery mechanism and does not read Qadira's internal operations WhatsApp group.

# Explainable Intelligence

Groundtruth is designed around explainable signals.

Every important flag should answer four questions:

1. **What happened?**
2. **Why was it flagged?**
3. **How material is it?**
4. **What should management review?**

For example:

```text
Deal Risk

No contact recorded for 24 days.
Last recorded interaction included a price objection.

Recommended:
Review the opportunity with the account owner.
```

This is intentionally different from presenting an unexplained:

```text
AI Risk Score: 82%
```

The purpose is to create a management conversation around an observable business fact.

---

# Human-in-the-Loop Guardrails

Groundtruth does not automatically execute business decisions.

The system produces signals and recommendations for human review.

This is particularly important for:

* Broker performance
* Deal risk
* Commission decisions
* Pipeline intervention

The operating model is:

```text
Signal
  ↓
Human Review
  ↓
Validate / Investigate
  ↓
Management Decision
  ↓
Action
```

Broker and deal-risk signals should initially be treated as review prompts rather than automatic actions until sufficient historical validation has been established.

# Salesforce Integration

Groundtruth is designed to operate using a read-only Salesforce integration.

### Integration

**Standard Salesforce API**

### Access

**Named read-only integration user**

### Data scope

Only the Salesforce objects and fields required by the four intelligence modules.

### Groundtruth does not:

* Modify Salesforce configuration
* Modify Salesforce objects
* Require new sales fields
* Read Qadira's internal operations WhatsApp group
* Pool Qadira's data with other clients
* Replace Northbay's Salesforce support responsibilities

This architecture allows Groundtruth to remain an independent intelligence layer while preserving the existing Salesforce operating model.

---

# Data Confidentiality

Client data is designed to remain isolated.

Qadira's underlying data is not intended to be:

* Shared with other customers
* Pooled across clients
* Used for cross-client benchmarking
* Aggregated with other developer data
* Used to create external benchmarks without explicit approval

Groundtruth outputs generated for Qadira belong to Qadira.

Data residency, retention and other contractual requirements would be confirmed during the discovery phase before production deployment.

This reflects the underlying cost and value driver because each project has its own:

* Pipeline
* Inventory
* Unit mix
* Price bands
* Broker footprint
* Calibration requirements

The goal is to push intelligence rather than make management search for it.

### Not another AI add-on

The solution operates outside Salesforce and focuses on four specific operational problems.

### Not a data-entry programme

### Not a Salesforce replacement

### Not a general BI platform

---

# Product Principles

## 1. Push, Don't Pull

Management should receive the important signal rather than search for it.

## 2. No New Data Entry

The solution should derive value from information the organisation already captures.

## 3. Explain Every Flag

Every important signal should have an understandable reason behind it.

## 4. Human Decision, Machine Signal

Groundtruth identifies what deserves attention. People remain responsible for the decision.

## 5. Prove Before Scaling

The pilot validates the intelligence before the organisation commits to broader deployment.
